What I do

I design end-to-end product experiences in close partnership with cross-functional teams to ship high-quality, production-ready features that solve real user and business problems.

  • Partner with product and business teams to understand customer problems, business goals, and priorities, translating them into clear, end-to-end experiences.
  • Collaborate with research and data to validate flows and identify drop-offs, enabling informed design decisions and reducing rework.
  • Work closely with fellow designers through critiques and deep dives, valuing feedback to continuously improve the quality of the experience.
  • Facilitate design reviews with stakeholders using clear storytelling and high-fidelity prototypes to align early and iterate faster.
  • Collaborate with engineering to design scalable, feasible solutions, cover edge cases, and ship polished, real-world experiences.
  • Build and maintain design systems using reusable components and auto layout to ensure consistency and scalability.
  • Design self-assisted and intuitive journeys that reduce friction, improve usability, and support business outcomes.

I focus on building simple, intuitive experiences for complex systems, optimizing for conversion, usability, and long-term user trust.

How I approach design

I’m a problem-first product designer who focuses on simplifying complex experiences while ensuring every decision drives real impact.

  • I start with understanding the problem deeply — using data, user behavior, and context to identify friction points before jumping into solutions.
  • I design with a strong focus on conversion, usability, and reducing drop-offs, ensuring experiences are not just intuitive but also effective.
  • I think in systems, not screens, creating scalable solutions using reusable components and consistent patterns.
  • I design for real-world usage, considering edge cases, user intent, and how flows perform beyond the ideal scenario.
  • I respect business and technical constraints, balancing user needs with feasibility to ship practical, high-impact solutions.
  • I iterate continuously through feedback, testing, and post-launch learnings, refining experiences based on actual performance.
  • I focus on creating experiences that build trust, reduce friction, and guide users clearly toward their goals.
