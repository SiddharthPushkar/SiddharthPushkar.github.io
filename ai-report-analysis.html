AI Report Analysis

Post-Booking Test Journey

WelUp is a healthcare startup providing preventive diagnostic services tailored for corporate employees. While the booking and delivery of blood test reports are automated, users struggle to interpret the results once they arrive in the app.

Role
UX Designer
Timeline
1 week
Team
Solo

The Problem

For most patients, blood test reports feel like reading a foreign language. They’re filled with medical jargon, numbers, and ranges that offer no clear meaning without a doctor’s help.

Instead of feeling informed, users feel confused and anxious. Unsure what’s normal or alarming. Reports designed for doctors fail to guide everyday users, leaving them dependent on others and disconnected from their own health data.

  • Medical Jargon
  • Friction & Disengagement
  • Ineffective Design

The User

Understanding who we’re designing for

Goals

  • Understand their health status without medical expertise
  • Identify actionable steps for improving health markers
  • Track health progress over time
  • Reduce anxiety about test results

Pain Points

  • Reports are intimidating and difficult to interpret
  • Red alerts cause unnecessary panic
  • Need to schedule consultation to understand results
  • No context on what normal variations mean

Key Research Insights

  • Users prefer plain-language summaries over detailed medical terminology, even if it means sacrificing some technical accuracy.
  • Context matters more than numbers, users want to know “what does this mean for me?” rather than just seeing values and ranges.
  • Visual hierarchy reduces anxiety, softer color coding and progressive disclosure help users process information without panic.
