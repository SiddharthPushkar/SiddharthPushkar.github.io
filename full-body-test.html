Problem Statement

How might we make full-body test booking understandable for employees so they can recognize their health needs and take full advantage of the services provided by their employer?

Employees were experiencing a confusing medical “puzzle” that prevented them from accessing valuable health benefits already paid for by their employer.

Planned it in 3 phases

Empathise

I ran two interview sessions

  • Interview for empathy to understand the current frustration and pain points, market trends and competitor experiences
  • And the other was an explorative interview, which helped understand user wants and needs by asking 5W+H questions
  • And SWOT analysis

Select Patient

This flow was a tricky part because it needed to understand the user’s mental model the most.

Map out the 7 stages of action to understand the mental model

In both the cases the center point was the mother (consumer), and the choice for selecting the test is based upon the consumer.

So I created the flow by understanding this mental model.

Address
