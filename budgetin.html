About BudgetIn

Level Up The Finances: A Budgeting App for Young Adults

Take control of your money. This innovative budgeting app empowers young adults to develop financial literacy and make smart spending decisions.

Actionable Insights: Gain clear visibility into your income and expenses, identify areas for potential savings, and optimize your budget for your unique financial situation.

Goal-Oriented Planning: Set realistic savings goals and track progress towards financial milestones. Integrated educational resources provide bite-sized financial knowledge, empowering you with the tools to manage your money effectively.

This app is the personalized financial coach, guiding people towards a secure and prosperous future.

Problem Statement

  • Young adults rely heavily on digital payments nowadays, which makes it hard to keep track of how much they spend.
  • This lack of transparency can lead to impulsive buying and difficulty in saving money effectively.
  • Existing budgeting tools might not be engaging or suited to the needs of young adults who are managing their finances mainly online.
  • Without effective tools, it’s challenging for young people to stick to budgets and manage their money wisely in the digital age.
